### Slide X — Commercialization & Real-World Impact
**Title:** Pathway to Impact: Commercialization & Stakeholder Value

**Visual:** A clean 3-pillar layout or 3 horizontal boxes showing: `1. The Product` → `2. Target Market` → `3. Revenue Strategy`. (Include a screenshot of the "Grid Pulse EV" dashboard).

**Slide Text:**
- **The Core Product:** **"Grid Pulse EV"** — an integrated AI-driven decision-support web dashboard for long-term EV demand forecasting, grid risk assessment, and scenario planning.
- **Target B2B Market (Who needs this?):**
  - **CEB & LECO (State Utilities):** For proactive transformer sizing, peak demand management, and grid upgrade planning across 25 districts.
  - **Government & Policy Makers (SLSEA / UDA):** For evaluating national EV adoption targets against actual grid capacity limits.
  - **Private Charging Operators (CPOs):** (e.g., ChargeNet) To understand district-level energy profiles and peak-hour risks before deploying infrastructure.
- **Proposed Value & Revenue Model:**
  - **Tier 1 (Public/National Good):** Basic scenario planning access provided to government utility planners to ensure national grid stability.
  - **Tier 2 (Commercial Subscription):** Premium B2B access for Private CPOs and large real-estate developers to access hyper-local demand forecasting and granular load profiles.
  - **Consultancy Services:** Custom EV grid-impact assessments for new residential and commercial mega-projects (e.g., Port City).

**Speaker Notes:** 
*"Research must translate into real-world value. Our commercialization strategy centers on our final product: the Grid Pulse EV SaaS platform. This dashboard provides unprecedented foresight into how EV adoption will stress the national grid. Our target market is strictly B2B. On one side, state utilities like the CEB need our long-term demand and risk models to upgrade transformers before they overload. On the other side, government policy makers need our scenario planning tools. We propose a hybrid revenue model: we secure national grid stability by offering core dashboard access to state utilities, while financially sustaining the project by selling premium subscriptions and grid-impact consultancy to private charging networks and large-scale real estate developers."*
